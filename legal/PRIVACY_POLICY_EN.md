## 1. Introduction

Phi-Bit ("we", "the Company") develops and operates the **Maa** apps for water plant customers and workers in Iraq. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and when we share it.

By using either Maa app, you agree to this policy.

## 2. Data We Collect

### a. Information you provide
- Full name
- Phone number (with SMS OTP for verification)
- Home address (district + details)
- WhatsApp number (optional)

### b. Device data (with your permission)
- **Location (GPS)**:
  - **Customer**: Once at registration to record your home location. We do NOT track you afterwards.
  - **Worker**: During work shifts only to confirm deliveries and calculate commissions. Tracking stops on logout.
- **Camera**: Worker only — to photograph the filled tank as proof.
- **Notifications**: For monthly refill reminders and order confirmations.

### c. Automatic data
- Usage analytics (which screens, anonymized)
- Device type, OS version, app version (for diagnostics)
- Crash reports

## 3. How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Data Used |
|---|---|
| Deliver water to your home | Name, phone, address, GPS |
| Monthly refill reminders | Phone/WhatsApp |
| Resolve delivery disputes | Worker GPS + photo proof |
| Calculate worker salaries | Refill count + work-shift GPS |
| Improve the app | Aggregated usage data |
| Customer support | Your info when you contact us |

## 4. Data Sharing

**We do NOT sell your personal data to anyone.**

We share limited data only with:

| Party | Data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Your registered water plant | Name, phone, address, refill history | To serve you |
| WhatsApp / SMS providers | Phone + message text | Reminders |
| Google Maps | GPS coords only (no identity) | Map display |
| Payment providers (ZainCash, etc.) | Amount + transaction ID only | Process payment |

## 5. Your Rights

- **Access**: Request a copy of your data anytime.
- **Correction**: Edit your name/address inside the app.
- **Deletion**: Email support@phi-bit.com to delete your account — we erase everything within 30 days.
- **Decline location**: You can deny GPS but drivers may not find you accurately.
- **Stop notifications**: From app or phone settings.

## 6. Data Security

- All sensitive data (phones, passwords) is **encrypted** on servers.
- All app-server traffic is **TLS 1.3 (HTTPS) encrypted**.
- Passwords are **hashed** and never readable by us.
- **Daily backups** and audit logs.

## 7. Children

This app is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data. If discovered, the account is immediately deleted.

## 8. Policy Changes

When this policy updates we notify you via:
- In-app notice
- WhatsApp to your registered number

Continued use after the update means you accept it.
