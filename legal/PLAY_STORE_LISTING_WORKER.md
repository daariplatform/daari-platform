### تبرير الموقع الخلفي (مهم جداً لمراجعة Google)

```
This app requires background location to fulfill its core purpose:
proving water deliveries to customer addresses and calculating accurate
commissions for delivery workers. Location tracking is active only during
work shifts (started manually by the worker) and stops automatically when
the worker logs out or ends their shift. This is essential for:

1. Proving the worker actually reached the customer's location (anti-fraud).
2. Calculating per-delivery commissions based on completed visits.
3. Plant managers monitoring their fleet in real-time.

Workers are clearly informed before each shift starts. A persistent
notification reminds them tracking is active. The data is NEVER shared
with third parties — only the plant the worker is employed by sees it.
```
